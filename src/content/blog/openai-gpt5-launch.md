OpenAI has officially unveiled GPT-5, its most capable language model to date. Announced at a packed event in San Francisco, GPT-5 represents a significant leap in AI reasoning, multimodal understanding, and real-world task completion.

Here's a breakdown of everything announced and what it means for the AI landscape.

## Key Announcements

### Native Multimodal Reasoning

Unlike GPT-4's bolted-on vision capabilities, GPT-5 was trained from the ground up as a multimodal model. It can seamlessly process and reason across text, images, audio, video, and code in a single conversation.

This means you can share a video of a bug in your app, and GPT-5 will watch it, understand the issue, and suggest code fixes — all in one turn.

### 1M Token Context Window

GPT-5 ships with a **1 million token context window** as standard. That's roughly 750,000 words — enough to process entire codebases, research paper collections, or book series in a single conversation.

### Real-Time Collaboration

A new feature called "Canvas 2.0" allows multiple users to collaborate with GPT-5 simultaneously on documents, code, and creative projects. Think Google Docs, but with an AI collaborator that actively participates.

### Improved Reasoning

OpenAI claims GPT-5 scores **92% on the ARC-AGI benchmark** (up from 76% for GPT-4). In our early testing, the model demonstrates noticeably better logical reasoning, mathematical problem-solving, and common-sense understanding.

## Pricing

GPT-5 is available through ChatGPT Plus ($25/month) and the API:

## What This Means for the Industry

GPT-5's launch puts pressure on every other AI lab. Google's Gemini Ultra 2 is expected later this year, and Anthropic's Claude 4 is reportedly in testing. The AI capability race shows no signs of slowing down.

For developers, the 1M token context and improved reasoning open up entirely new application categories:

- **Full codebase analysis** without chunking or RAG
- **Long-form content generation** with perfect consistency
- **Complex multi-step workflows** with fewer errors

## Our Take

GPT-5 is genuinely impressive, but it's also expensive. The jump from GPT-4o to GPT-5 is significant for complex tasks, but for simpler use cases, GPT-4o and GPT-5 Mini offer better value.
